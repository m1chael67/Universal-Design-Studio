# West Coast frame tent 3D reference notes

The default 3D style is now **West Coast frame / hipped roof**, not a gable-roof structure.

## References checked

1. [Tent and Table — West Coast Frame Assembly, 10 and 20 ft wide](https://www.tentandtable.net/media/tnt/instructions/Assembly_WCF_10w_20w_rev3-NN.pdf). Shows the 20 x 40 reference overhead, 10-foot modular spans, side tees, spreaders, four hip rafters, ridge crowns and corner fittings. The published 20 x 40 parts list includes four 14 ft 4 in hip rafters, eight 10 ft 6 in rafters, fourteen 9 ft 4 in spreaders and twelve legs.
2. [Dremcor — 20 x 40 West Coast frame tent installation](https://www.dremcor.com/content/file/Instructionmanual-20x40WestCoastframetent-Dremcor.pdf). Corroborates the four hip rafters, intermediate ridge crowns and spreaders.
3. [Moonwalk USA — West Coast 20 x 40](https://moonwalkusa.com/products/west-coast-frame-tent-20x40). Lists nominal 7-foot legs, two-inch aluminum tubing and frame member lengths.

## Model scope and assumptions

- West Coast is the default 3D style. The original gable, high-gable and flat modes remain optional conceptual profiles.
- Standard roof uses a four-sided hipped canopy and a ridge shortened at each end, with diagonal hip rafters and ridge crowns. The 20-foot-wide conceptual rise is five feet, so 7-foot eaves produce a 12-foot ridge.
- Main long-axis module is 10 feet when the selected length permits. If the user specifies a nonstandard length, the 3D model divides it evenly into approximately 10-foot bays. This is a visual approximation, **not** a valid pipe cut list.
- Nominal overall geometry and tubing centers differ from manufactured pipe cut lengths because fittings, sockets and connector offsets consume length. Do **not** substitute rendered member lengths for manufacturer pipe specifications.
- Published manuals cover particular product lines and sizes. A West Coast frame from another manufacturer or a 30/40-foot-wide frame, twin-tube system or sectional system may require different crowns, rafters, trusses and dimensions. No claim of universal exactness.
- Site/3D planner is **not** a structural design, anchoring plan or installation instruction. Wind, snow, ballast and engineering loads are not calculated.
- Sidewalls, doors, interior furniture and walk-through bounds are drawn from the project's saved interior layout.
- For exact Universal Rentals inventory accuracy, a future manufacturer-specific parts/profile catalog should identify tent brand/model and stock component lengths for each size.

## Walk-through

Walk-through keeps camera and target inside a safety margin of the selected tent footprint, disables wheel zoom and panning, limits orbit distance and adds 2-foot navigation buttons. The bounds apply to the individual-tent view, not the entire-site overview.
